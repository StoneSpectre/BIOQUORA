## 1. Architectural Mission
`BioGraphAI v1.0` implements **Step 5 Stage 11 (Graph Machine Learning & Geometric AI Platform)** of the Bioquora Founder Bible. It transforms Bioquora from an integrated knowledge ecosystem and simulation platform into an active, deep learning AI discovery engine using GNNs and representation learning directly over biology.

---

## 2. Core Architectural Layers
1. **Graph Embeddings & Networks:** `KnowledgeGraphEmbeddings` for vector spaces, `GraphNeuralNetworks` for prediction, and `GeometricDeepLearning` for 3D structures.
2. **Learning & Representation:** `SelfSupervisedGraphLearning` for zero-label data, `MultiModalGraphLearning` for heterogeneous fusion, and `GraphFoundationModels` for zero-shot capabilities.
3. **Retrieval & Reasoning:** `ScientificRetrievalEngine` for GraphRAG, and `GraphReasoningEngine` for multi-hop mechanistic explanations.
4. **Discovery & Explainability:** `ScientificDiscoveryEngine` generates novel targets, while `ExplainableAIFramework` offers GNN explanations and subgraph analysis.
5. **Infrastructure:** `GraphAnalyticsDashboard`, `AITrainingInfrastructure`, `GraphAIRepository`, and a suite of `Validation` / `BenchmarkSuite` ensuring OpenBioLink / OGB compliance.
