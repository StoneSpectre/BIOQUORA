## 1. Architectural Mission
`BioCompute Core v1.0` implements **Step 5 Stage 1 (Computational Biology Foundations)** of the Bioquora Founder Bible. It establishes the mathematical, biological, computational, and systems-level foundations required to model living organisms computationally.
Unlike observational biology, BioCompute Core transforms `BioGraph v1.0` entities into executable kinetic, stochastic, structural, and differential equation models capable of in-silico simulation, hypothesis testing, and multi-scale biological reasoning.

---

## 2. Core Architectural Layers
1. **Biological Knowledge & Foundations Layer:** `MolecularBiologyEngine`, `CellularBiologyEngine`, `GeneticsGenomicEngine`, and `ProteinBiologyEngine` formalize central dogma kinetics, cell cycle states, Mendelian/Hardy-Weinberg genetics, and enzyme kinetics (`Michaelis-Menten`).
2. **Mathematical Biology & Scientific Computing Layer:** `MathematicalBiologyLibrary` provides quantitative solvers for ODEs (`Runge-Kutta`), PDEs, and stochastic kinetics (`Gillespie SSA`), powered by `ScientificComputingFramework` sparse arrays and GPU acceleration.
3. **Systems Biology & Multi-Scale Layer:** `BiologicalNetworkTheoryEngine` models scale-free power-law topologies, `SystemsBiologyFoundationsEngine` simulates feedback loops and homeostasis, and `MultiScaleBiologyEngine` links 12 biological scales (`Atom -> Ecosystem`).
4. **Experimentation & Workflow Layer:** `ComputationalExperimentDesignEngine` orchestrates 5-stage in-silico experiments (`Hypothesis -> Model -> Simulation -> Analysis -> Validation`), governed by `ScientificWorkflowEngine` DAG pipelines and `ReproducibleComputationalScience` SHA-256 locking.
5. **Step 4 Knowledge Graph Bridge:** `Step4KnowledgeGraphIntegration` seamlessly connects canonical `BioGraph v1.0` nodes (`BioQ-IDs`) into parameter sets for computational simulation.
