## 1. Architectural Mission
`BioSystems v1.0` implements **Step 5 Stage 5 (Systems Biology Platform & Dynamic Biological Systems Intelligence)** of the Bioquora Founder Bible. It models, simulates, analyzes, and predicts the behavior of complex biological systems across multiple spatial and temporal scales.
Rather than treating genes, proteins, pathways, and cells as isolated entities, BioSystems models them as interconnected dynamical systems exhibiting emergence, feedback, robustness, adaptation, and evolution.

---

## 2. Core Architectural Layers
1. **Systems Principles & Multi-Scale Hierarchy:** `SystemsBiologyArchitecture` defines emergence, robustness, modularity, and closed-loop feedback control. `MultiScaleModelingEngine` couples 5 spatial-temporal strata: Atomic -> Molecular -> Cellular -> Tissue -> Whole Organism.
2. **Mathematical Formalisms & Solvers:** `MathematicalBiologyEngine` integrates ODEs, PDEs, DDEs, SDEs, and Boolean state switches. `BiologicalControlEngine` analyzes bistable toggle switches and biological oscillators.
3. **Digital Cell, Tissue & Homeostasis Platforms:** `DigitalCellFramework` simulates whole-cell organelle compartmentalization, membrane transport, and ATP/NADH energy balance. `TissueSystemsEngine` models spatial cell-cell vascular gradients, while `BiologicalHomeostasisPlatform` maintains physiological stability under perturbation.
4. **Perturbations, Adaptation & State Space Trajectories:** `SystemsPerturbationEngine` models CRISPR knockouts and small-molecule inhibition. `BiologicalStateSpaceEngine` tracks attractor basins across Healthy -> Disease -> Treatment -> Recovery paths.
5. **Systems AI, Analytics & KG Loop:** `SystemsAIPlatform` applies Neural ODEs / PINNs for forecasting, `SystemsAnalyticsDashboard` computes Lyapunov stability and thermodynamic entropy, and `SystemsKGIntegrationLayer` closes the loop with `BioGraph v1.0` and `BioNetwork v1.0`.
