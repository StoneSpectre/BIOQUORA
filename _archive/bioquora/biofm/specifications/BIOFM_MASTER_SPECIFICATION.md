# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE — STEP 5 STAGE 15 MASTER SPECIFICATION

## Codename: BioFM v1.0
**Mission:** Develop a multimodal Biological Foundation Model that learns a unified representation of biology from heterogeneous scientific data, enabling reasoning, prediction, retrieval, generation, and scientific discovery across the entire Bioquora ecosystem.

### Core Modules
1. **Foundation Model Architecture:** Designed the core multimodal AI architecture.
2. **Biological Tokenization:** Converts biological information into AI-readable representations.
3. **Multimodal Encoder:** Encodes every biological modality (DNA, RNA, proteins, graphs, pathways, images, clinical records, omics, simulation outputs).
4. **Unified Embedding Space:** Maps all biological entities into one latent space.
5. **Self-Supervised Pretraining:** Learns without extensive manual labels (Masked Sequence Modeling, Contrastive Learning).
6. **Scientific Reasoning Engine:** Enables multi-step biological reasoning (Mechanistic, Causal, Hypothesis).
7. **Retrieval-Augmented Biology (BioRAG):** Grounds model outputs in trusted evidence.
8. **Scientific Generation:** Generates biologically meaningful outputs (Summaries, Hypotheses, Experimental Plans).
9. **Biological Agents:** Creates autonomous AI agents (Literature, Protein, Drug Discovery).
10. **Scientific Planning:** Assists researchers in experimental design.
11. **Fine-Tuning Platform:** Adapts BioFM to specialized tasks.
12. **Evaluation Framework:** Measures biological intelligence.
13. **Safety & Reliability:** Improves trustworthiness (Hallucination Rate, Citation Accuracy).
14. **Model Serving Infrastructure:** Deploys BioFM efficiently.
15. **APIs:** Foundation Model API, Embedding API, BioRAG API, Agent API, etc.
16. **Storage Architecture:** Stores checkpoints, embeddings, training datasets.
17. **Validation:** Validates cross-modal generalization and reasoning consistency.
18. **Benchmarking:** Benchmarks against ESM, Geneformer, scGPT, AlphaFold.
19. **Integration:** Integrates BioFM with BioGraph, BioNetwork, BioSystems, BioPathway, BioStructure, BioProteinAI, BioOmics, BioSim, and BioPrecision.

### Conclusion
BioFM becomes the central intelligence engine of Bioquora, establishing the foundation for Stage 16 – Experimental AI & Autonomous Science.
