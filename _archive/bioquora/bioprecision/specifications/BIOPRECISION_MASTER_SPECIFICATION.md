## 1. Architectural Mission
`BioPrecision v1.0` implements **Step 5 Stage 14 (Disease Modeling & Precision Medicine Platform)**. It evolves Bioquora into a precision medicine operating system capable of predicting how a specific disease behaves in a specific patient, constructing digital twins, and generating individualized therapeutic recommendations.

---

## 2. Core Architectural Layers
1. **Patient Representation & Digital Twins:** `PatientDigitalTwinEngine` and `PatientRepresentationModel` construct unified models integrating multi-omics, clinical data, imaging, and longitudinal records.
2. **Disease & Risk Modeling:** `DiseaseStateEngine` scales disease states, while `RiskIntelligenceEngine` and `PreventiveHealthEngine` estimate future risks and preventive strategies.
3. **Personalized Therapy & Clinical Support:** `TherapyPredictionPlatform`, `PersonalizedBiomarkerEngine`, and `ClinicalDecisionEngine` assist with precise, evidence-based interventions.
4. **Longitudinal & Population Intelligence:** `LongitudinalIntelligencePlatform` tracks evolution over time, and `PopulationIntelligencePlatform` enables subgroup discovery.
5. **Explainability & Analytics:** `ExplainabilityFramework` provides transparent evidence, visualized via `PrecisionAnalyticsDashboard`.
6. **Infrastructure:** Supported by `TrainingPlatform`, `PrecisionMedicineServices` (APIs), `PrecisionRepository` (databases), and rigorous validation on MIMIC-IV and UK Biobank.
