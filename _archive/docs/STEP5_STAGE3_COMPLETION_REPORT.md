## 2. Core Engines & Platforms Delivered
1. **Reference Assembly & Alignment Layer:** Manages canonical reference genomes (`ReferenceGenomePlatform` for GRCh38, T2T-CHM13, GRCm39) and orchestrates short/long-read alignments (`GenomeAssemblyAlignmentEngine`).
2. **Annotation & Functional Genomics:** Annotates GENCODE v46 / RefSeq features (`GenomeAnnotationEngine`) and integrates functional regulatory networks, eQTLs, ATAC-seq chromatin peaks, and histone modifications (`FunctionalGenomicsPlatform`).
3. **Variant Discovery & Clinical Interpretation:** Calls SNPs, Indels, CNVs, and SVs (`VariantDiscoveryCallingEngine`) and interprets biological/clinical impact using ClinVar pathogenicity, ACMG guidelines, gnomAD allele frequencies, and CADD/AlphaMissense (`VariantInterpretationPlatform`).
4. **Comparative, Population & Evolutionary Genomics:** Analyzes orthology and synteny blocks across species (`ComparativeGenomicsEngine`), calculates linkage disequilibrium ($r^2$), global MAF, and population differentiation (`PopulationGenomicsPlatform`), and models evolutionary conservation (`EvolutionaryGenomicsEngine`).
5. **AI Embeddings & KG Bridge:** Generates 1024D Nucleotide Transformer & Enformer vectors (`GenomeEmbeddingService`), predicts variant effects via deep learning (`AIForGenomicsPlatform`), and connects genomic loci directly to `BioGraph v1.0` (`GenomeKnowledgeGraphIntegration`).
