2. **Clinical Translation:** `BiomarkerDiscoveryEngine` (prognostic, diagnostic) and `PatientSubtypingPlatform` for patient stratification based on multi-omics graph clustering.

5. **AI & Clinical Decision:** `DiseaseAIPlatform` for survival/outcome predictions, paired with `ClinicalExplainabilityEngine` for mechanistic reasoning.
6. **Infrastructure:** `DiseaseSimilarityEngine`, `NetworkMedicineDashboard`, `NetworkMedicineServices` (APIs), and a robust validation/benchmarking suite against Open Targets and DisGeNET.
