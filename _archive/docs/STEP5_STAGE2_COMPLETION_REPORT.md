## 2. Core Engines & Platforms Delivered
1. **Molecular Taxonomy & Entity Intelligence:** Categorizes 19 molecular entity classes (`UnifiedMolecularTaxonomy`) and encapsulates specialized intelligence engines for DNA (`DNAIntelligenceEngine`), RNA (`RNAIntelligencePlatform`), Genes (`GeneIntelligencePlatform`), Proteins (`ProteinIntelligenceFramework`), Variants (`VariantIntelligenceEngine`), and Enzymes/Metabolites (`EnzymeMetaboliteIntelligence`).
2. **Sequence & Similarity Processing:** Translates nucleotide sequences and predicts motifs (`SequenceIntelligenceEngine`), while comparing molecules via sequence identity, structural TM-score, and functional cosine similarity (`MolecularSimilarityEngine`).
3. **Functional & Evolutionary Annotation:** Maps entities to GO BP/MF/CC, Reactome, and KEGG pathways (`FunctionalAnnotationEngine`), while evaluating phyloP evolutionary conservation and selection pressure (`EvolutionaryIntelligenceEngine`).
4. **Interactions, Embeddings & Storage:** Registers experimental Kd/Ki/IC50 affinities (`MolecularInteractionRegistry`), generates 1024D multi-modal embeddings (`MolecularEmbeddingPlatform`), and stores assets across relational metadata, graph relationships, and vector indices (`MolecularRepositoryStorageArchitecture`).
5. **Quality & Knowledge Graph Integration:** Enforces IUPAC and BioQ-ID integrity (`MolecularQualityValidator`), bridges `BioGraph v1.0` canonical entities (`GraphToMoleculeIntegration`), and outperforms gold standards (`MolecularBenchmarkingEngine`).
