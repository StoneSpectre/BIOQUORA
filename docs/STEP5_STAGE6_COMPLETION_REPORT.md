## 2. Core Engines & Platforms Delivered
1. **Pathway Construction & Mechanistic Reactions:** Implements automated pathway construction from `BioGraph v1.0` and `BioNetwork v1.0` (`AutomaticPathwayBuilder`) and models reaction mechanisms like phosphorylation, ubiquitination, methylation, and cleavage (`MechanisticBiologyEngine`).
2. **Domain Pathway Intelligence Engines:**
   - **Signaling (`SignalTransductionPlatform`):** MAPK, PI3K-AKT-mTOR, JAK-STAT, NF-kB, Notch, Wnt, Hedgehog, Hippo, TGF-beta.
   - **Metabolic (`MetabolicPathwayEngine`):** Glycolysis, TCA, OxPhos, Pentose Phosphate, Lipid/Amino Acid metabolism.
   - **Regulatory (`GeneRegulatoryPathwaysEngine`):** TF regulons, miRNA repression, chromatin loops.
   - **Immune (`ImmunePathwayPlatform`):** Innate/Adaptive cascades, Cytokine loops, TCR co-stimulation, checkpoint inhibitors.
   - **Disease (`DiseaseMechanismModelingEngine`):** Traces causal mechanism chains from mutation -> network failure -> clinical manifestation.
3. **Simulation, Causal Inference & AI Platform:** Evaluates dynamic interventions (`DynamicPathwaySimulator`), infers root causes (`CausalPathwayReasoningEngine`), and predicts missing orphan reactions (`AIForPathwayBiologyPlatform`).
