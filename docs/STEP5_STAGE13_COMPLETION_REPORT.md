## 2. Core Engines & Platforms Delivered
1. **Target Intelligence:** `TargetDiscoveryEngine` and `TargetValidationPlatform` to identify and clinically validate biological targets.
2. **Molecular Libraries & Screening:** `MolecularLibraryPlatform` managing millions of compounds, and `AIVirtualScreening` for rapid docking and hit identification.
3. **Molecular Design:** `StructureBasedDesignPlatform` and `LigandDesignEngine` for targeted drug design.
4. **Generative AI:** `MoleculeGenerationPlatform` to invent novel, synthesizable, and non-toxic drug candidates from scratch.
5. **Pharmacology & Toxicology:** `LeadOptimizationEngine`, `ADMETPlatform`, and `ToxicologyIntelligence` to refine pharmacokinetics and safety profiles.
6. **Translation & Ranking:** `DrugRepurposingIntelligence` to find new uses for old drugs, `ClinicalTranslationPlatform`, and `CandidatePrioritization` to finalize the development pipeline.
7. **Infrastructure:** APIs (`DrugDiscoveryServices`), robust data storage (`DrugDiscoveryRepository`), and rigorous benchmarking against DUD-E and MoleculeNet.
