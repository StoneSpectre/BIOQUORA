# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.47 — HMDB (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: The Human Metabolome Database (HMDB), developed by the University of Alberta, is the world's most comprehensive resource for human metabolites, biofluids, tissues, metabolic pathways, diseases, exposome, concentrations, and spectral data (NMR/MS). While KEGG focuses on pathways and ChEBI focuses on chemical ontology, HMDB focuses specifically on the human metabolome. For Bioquora, HMDB becomes the Human Metabolomics Intelligence Layer, enabling AI to reason from metabolites → pathways → tissues → diseases → drugs → biomarkers.
