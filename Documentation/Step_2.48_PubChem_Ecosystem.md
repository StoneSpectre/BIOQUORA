# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.48 — PubChem (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: PubChem, maintained by the National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI), is the world's largest open chemical information repository. It integrates chemical structures, bioassays, biological activities, genes, proteins, pathways, patents, safety data, vendors, literature, and biomedical annotations. For Bioquora, PubChem becomes the Universal Chemical Intelligence Layer, connecting molecules → targets → assays → diseases → drugs → publications.

### 2. Core Collections (Harvest EVERYTHING)
* **PubChem Compound (CID)**: Chemical structures, Drug molecules, Natural products, Metabolites, Lipids, Peptides, Environmental chemicals, Food compounds, Cosmetics, Industrial chemicals.
* **PubChem Substance (SID)**: Vendor compounds, Depositor records, Laboratory chemicals, Commercial chemicals, Patent compounds, Screening collections.

* **Biological Targets**: Genes, Proteins, Enzymes, Receptors, Ion channels, Transporters, GPCRs, Kinases.
