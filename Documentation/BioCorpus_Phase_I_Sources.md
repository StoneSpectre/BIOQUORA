# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## PHASE I — GLOBAL BIOMEDICAL ORGANIZATIONS, STANDARDS, REGISTRIES & REGULATORY INFRASTRUCTURE

These are the organizations whose data, standards, and publications should continuously feed Bioquora.

---

### 1. World Health Organization (WHO)
* **Official**: [who.int](https://www.who.int)
* **Resources**: Global Health Observatory, ICD-11, Disease Guidelines, Essential Medicines List, TB Portal, HIV Portal, Malaria Portal, Vaccine Position Papers, Health Statistics, Publications, APIs.

### 2. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)
* **Official**: [cdc.gov](https://www.cdc.gov)
* **Resources**: CDC WONDER, NHANES, NNDSS, FluView, COVID Data Tracker, BRFSS, Vital Statistics, Publications.

### 3. National Institutes of Health (NIH)
* **Official**: [nih.gov](https://www.nih.gov)
* **Resources**: NIH Reporter, NIH RePORTER API, Clinical Center, Common Fund, Data Sharing, Open Citation.

### 4. FDA
* **Official**: [fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov)
* **Developer**: [open.fda.gov](https://open.fda.gov)
* **Resources**: Drugs@FDA, OpenFDA API, Orange Book, Purple Book, FAERS, Device Approvals, Medical Devices, Drug Labels.

### 5. European Medicines Agency (EMA)
* **Official**: [ema.europa.eu](https://www.ema.europa.eu)
* **Resources**: EPAR, Clinical Data, Medicines Database, Pharmacovigilance.

### 6. PMDA (Japan)
* **Official**: [pmda.go.jp](https://www.pmda.go.jp)
* **Resources**: Drug approvals, Safety reports, Clinical information.

### 7. MHRA (United Kingdom)
* **Official**: [gov.uk/mhra](https://www.gov.uk/mhra)
* **Resources**: Drug approvals, Safety alerts, Yellow Card.

### 8. Health Canada
* **Official**: [canada.ca/en/health-canada.html](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada.html)
* **Resources**: Drug Product Database, Clinical Information Portal.

### 9. TGA Australia
* **Official**: [tga.gov.au](https://www.tga.gov.au)
* **Resources**: ARTG, Medicine safety, Clinical approvals.

### 10. CDSCO India
* **Official**: [cdsco.gov.in](https://cdsco.gov.in)
* **Resources**: Drug approvals, Medical devices, Clinical trials.

### 11. Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR)
* **Official**: [icmr.gov.in](https://www.icmr.gov.in)
* **Resources**: Publications, Guidelines, COVID Research, Registries.

### 12. DBT India
* **Official**: [dbtindia.gov.in](https://dbtindia.gov.in)
* **Resources**: Biotechnology programs, National repositories, Research initiatives.

### 13. CSIR India
* **Official**: [csir.res.in](https://www.csir.res.in)
* **Resources**: Drug discovery, Genomics, Biotechnology.

### 14. INSACOG
* **Official**: [dbtindia.gov.in/insacog](https://dbtindia.gov.in/insacog)
* **Resources**: SARS-CoV-2 genomics, Variant surveillance.

### 15. APEDA (Food & Agricultural Health)
* **Official**: [apeda.gov.in](https://apeda.gov.in)
* **Resources**: Food safety, Export standards.
