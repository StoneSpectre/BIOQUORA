# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 1.20 — ORCID Ecosystem (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: ORCID (Open Researcher and Contributor ID) is the global standard for uniquely identifying researchers. It connects authors to publications, datasets, grants, affiliations, peer reviews, and software. For Bioquora, ORCID is the identity backbone of the biomedical knowledge graph.
