# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.59 — Ensembl (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: Ensembl, developed by the European Bioinformatics Institute (EMBL-EBI) and the Wellcome Sanger Institute, is one of the world's most comprehensive genome annotation platforms. Unlike NCBI Gene or RefSeq, Ensembl integrates high-quality genome assemblies, gene annotations, comparative genomics, regulatory elements, variation data, evolutionary conservation, and genome browsers across thousands of species. For Bioquora, Ensembl becomes the Genome Annotation & Comparative Genomics Intelligence Layer, enabling AI reasoning from genome → gene → transcript → protein → regulation → evolution → disease.
