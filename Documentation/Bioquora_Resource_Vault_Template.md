### The 6 Master Steps to Execute Under This Template:
* **Step 1 — BioLiterature**: Literature repositories, Publishers, Citation databases, Preprint servers, Open science repositories.
* **Step 2 — BioData**: Genomics, Proteomics, Clinical datasets, Imaging, Drug databases, Molecular databases, Public health datasets, Single-cell datasets, Multi-omics repositories.
* **Step 3 — BioAI**: Biomedical foundation models, LLMs, Protein AI, Vision models, Agent frameworks, Benchmarks, MLOps, Vector databases.
* **Step 4 — BioKnowledge**: Ontologies, Knowledge graphs, Controlled vocabularies, Terminologies, RDF/OWL resources, Graph databases.
* **Step 5 — BioWorld**: NIH, NCBI, EMBL-EBI, WHO, FDA, CDC, Broad Institute, Human Cell Atlas, HuBMAP, GA4GH, ELIXIR, Standards organizations.
* **Step 6 — BioCore**: ETL frameworks, Search infrastructure, APIs, Knowledge graph engine, Agent orchestration, Monitoring, Analytics, Deployment, Enterprise architecture.
