# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 1.37 — re3data Ecosystem (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: re3data (Registry of Research Data Repositories) is the world's largest registry of research data repositories, covering 3,500+ repositories across 150+ countries. It is jointly developed by Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), Helmholtz Open Science, and partners. For Bioquora, re3data is the global repository discovery layer, allowing automatic discovery of virtually every biomedical data repository.
