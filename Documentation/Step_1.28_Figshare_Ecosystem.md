# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 1.28 — Figshare Ecosystem (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: Figshare is one of the world's largest FAIR repositories for research datasets, figures, supplementary files, software, posters, videos, code, protocols, theses, and negative results. It is used by universities, publishers, governments, and research organizations worldwide. For Bioquora, Figshare provides a rich source of supplementary biomedical research objects that are often not available through traditional literature databases.
