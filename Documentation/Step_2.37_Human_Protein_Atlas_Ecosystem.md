# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.37 — Human Protein Atlas (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: The Human Protein Atlas (HPA), maintained by the SciLifeLab (Sweden) and collaborators, is the world's most comprehensive resource for protein expression, tissue localization, single-cell protein distribution, cancer proteomics, subcellular localization, blood proteins, and spatial proteomics. Unlike GTEx (RNA expression), HPA measures proteins—the functional molecules of biology. For Bioquora, HPA becomes the Proteomics & Protein Expression Intelligence Layer.

### 2. Core Collections (Harvest EVERYTHING)
* **Tissue Atlas**: Normal tissues, Protein expression, Immunohistochemistry (IHC), Organ-specific proteins.
* **Cell Atlas**: Subcellular localization, Cell line expression, Organelle localization, Protein trafficking.
* **Single Cell Type Atlas**: Single-cell RNA integration, Cell-type specificity, Protein expression, Immune cell atlas.
* **Pathology Atlas**: Cancer biomarkers, Tumor protein expression, Survival associations, Diagnostic biomarkers.
* **Brain Atlas**: Brain regions, Neurons, Glia, Brain-specific proteins.
* **Blood Atlas**: Plasma proteins, Secreted proteins, Immune proteins, Cytokines.
* **Metabolic Atlas**: Metabolic enzymes, Liver metabolism, Energy metabolism.
