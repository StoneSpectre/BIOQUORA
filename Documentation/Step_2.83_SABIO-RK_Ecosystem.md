# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.83 — SABIO-RK (System for the Analysis of Biochemical Pathways – Reaction Kinetics) (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: SABIO-RK, maintained by the Heidelberg Institute for Theoretical Studies (HITS), is the world's leading curated database of experimentally determined biochemical reaction kinetics. Unlike BRENDA (enzyme-centric kinetics), SABIO-RK focuses on individual biochemical reactions, providing rate equations, kinetic laws, reaction mechanisms, experimental conditions, mutant effects, and mathematical models. For Bioquora, SABIO-RK becomes the Reaction Kinetics & Dynamic Systems Biology Intelligence Layer, enabling AI reasoning from enzyme → reaction → kinetic model → pathway → metabolism → disease → therapeutic intervention.
