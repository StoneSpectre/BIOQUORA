# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.21 — ChEBI (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: ChEBI (Chemical Entities of Biological Interest) is the world's leading open chemical ontology and molecular knowledgebase, maintained by EMBL-EBI. It provides standardized identifiers, molecular structures, chemical roles, biological roles, ontology relationships, and cross-references for small molecules, metabolites, lipids, drugs, cofactors, ions, natural products, carbohydrates, peptides, and biomolecules. For Bioquora, ChEBI is the Chemical Intelligence Layer, connecting chemistry to biology, pharmacology, metabolism, diseases, and AI-driven molecular reasoning.
