# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.35 — CELLxGENE Discover (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: CELLxGENE Discover, developed by the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative (CZI), is one of the world's largest open repositories of curated single-cell datasets, containing hundreds of millions of annotated cells across healthy and diseased tissues. It provides the CELLxGENE Census, a unified, queryable atlas of single-cell transcriptomic data designed specifically for AI, foundation models, and large-scale biological discovery. For Bioquora, CELLxGENE becomes the Large-Scale Single-Cell Discovery Intelligence Layer.
