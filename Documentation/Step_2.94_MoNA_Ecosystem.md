# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.94 — MoNA (MassBank of North America) (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: MoNA (MassBank of North America), maintained by the Fiehn Lab at the University of California, Davis, is one of the world's largest aggregated mass spectral libraries, integrating spectra from MassBank, GNPS, HMDB, NIST, LipidBlast, and numerous community resources. Unlike MassBank (individual curated repository), MoNA acts as a federated spectral aggregation platform, enabling unified spectral searching, compound identification, and interoperability across metabolomics ecosystems. For Bioquora, MoNA becomes the Unified Spectral Knowledge Layer, enabling AI reasoning from reference spectrum → chemical identity → biological function → pathway → disease → therapeutic insight.
