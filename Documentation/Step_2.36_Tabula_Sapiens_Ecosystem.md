# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.36 — Tabula Sapiens (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: Tabula Sapiens, developed by the Chan Zuckerberg Biohub, is the most comprehensive healthy human single-cell reference atlas, profiling nearly every major organ at single-cell resolution. Unlike disease-focused atlases, Tabula Sapiens provides the baseline reference of normal human biology, making it indispensable for disease comparison, biomarker discovery, regenerative medicine, developmental biology, and AI foundation models. For Bioquora, Tabula Sapiens becomes the Healthy Human Cellular Reference Layer.
