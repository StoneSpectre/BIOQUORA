# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.95 — NIST Mass Spectral Library (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: The NIST Mass Spectral Library, developed by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST, USA), is the global gold standard for mass spectral identification. Unlike community repositories such as MoNA or MassBank, NIST provides highly curated, experimentally validated reference spectra used worldwide in analytical chemistry, metabolomics, environmental science, food chemistry, toxicology, pharmaceutical analysis, forensics, and clinical laboratories. For Bioquora, NIST becomes the Gold-Standard Spectral Validation Layer, enabling AI reasoning from validated reference spectrum → confirmed compound → biological role → pathway → disease → clinical interpretation.
