# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.86 — BioCyc (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: BioCyc, developed by SRI International, is the world's largest collection of organism-specific Pathway/Genome Databases (PGDBs). Unlike MetaCyc (a reference database), BioCyc contains tens of thousands of curated and computationally generated PGDBs, integrating genomes, metabolic pathways, enzymes, operons, transporters, regulatory networks, metabolites, and comparative genomics. For Bioquora, BioCyc becomes the Comparative Metabolism & Organism Intelligence Layer, enabling AI reasoning from genome → gene → operon → enzyme → reaction → pathway → phenotype → evolution.
