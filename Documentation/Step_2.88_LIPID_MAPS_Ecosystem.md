# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.88 — LIPID MAPS (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: LIPID MAPS, maintained by the LIPID MAPS Consortium, is the world's leading resource for lipid classification, lipid structures, lipid metabolism, lipidomics, lipid pathways, and mass spectrometry-based lipid identification. Unlike HMDB (general metabolomics) or ChEBI (chemical ontology), LIPID MAPS specializes exclusively in lipid biology, making it indispensable for research in metabolism, cardiovascular disease, neurobiology, inflammation, cancer, immunology, obesity, diabetes, and precision medicine. For Bioquora, LIPID MAPS becomes the Lipidomics & Membrane Biology Intelligence Layer, enabling AI reasoning from lipid → enzyme → metabolic pathway → cell membrane → signaling → disease → therapy.
