# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.69 — UniProt (Universal Protein Resource) (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: UniProt (Universal Protein Resource), maintained by the UniProt Consortium (EMBL-EBI, SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics, and PIR), is the world's most comprehensive and authoritative protein knowledgebase. Unlike RefSeq (reference sequences) or GO (functional ontology), UniProt integrates protein sequences, functions, structures, domains, post-translational modifications (PTMs), interactions, diseases, subcellular localization, pathways, and literature into a unified protein-centric platform. For Bioquora, UniProt becomes the Protein Intelligence & Structural Biology Layer, enabling AI reasoning from gene → protein → structure → function → pathway → disease → therapeutic target.
