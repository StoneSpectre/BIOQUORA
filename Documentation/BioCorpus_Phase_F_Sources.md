# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## PHASE F — BIOMEDICAL RESEARCH INFRASTRUCTURE (Tier-1 Global Scientific Repositories)

These are the repositories that large pharmaceutical companies, research institutes, and AI labs use to build biomedical intelligence systems.

---

### 1. EMBL–EBI COMPLETE ECOSYSTEM (≈50+ Resources)
*This is arguably the most important European biomedical infrastructure.*
* **Main Portal**: [EMBL–EBI](https://www.ebi.ac.uk)

**Genomics**
* [European Nucleotide Archive (ENA)](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena)
* [Ensembl Genome Browser](https://www.ensembl.org)
* [Ensembl Genomes](https://ensemblgenomes.org)
* [Expression Atlas](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gxa)
* [ArrayExpress Archive](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/arrayexpress)

**Proteomics**
* [UniProt](https://www.uniprot.org)
* [PRIDE Archive](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride)
* [InterPro](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/interpro)
* [Pfam](https://pfam.xfam.org)

**Structural Biology**
* [PDBe (Protein Data Bank Europe)](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pdbe)
* [AlphaFold Protein Structure Database](https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk)
* [EMPIAR](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/empiar)

**Chemistry**
* [ChEMBL](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl)
* [ChEBI](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi)

**Metabolomics**
* [MetaboLights](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metabolights)

**Single-cell**
* [Single Cell Expression Atlas](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gxa/sc/home)

**BioImages**
* [BioImage Archive](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/bioimage-archive)
* [Image Data Resource (IDR)](https://idr.openmicroscopy.org)

**Metadata**
* [BioStudies](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies) — Study-level metadata and links to supporting datasets across EMBL-EBI repositories. It currently indexes millions of studies and tens of millions of files.
* [BioSamples](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biosamples) — Centralized biological sample metadata with APIs and standardized identifiers for linking datasets across archives.
