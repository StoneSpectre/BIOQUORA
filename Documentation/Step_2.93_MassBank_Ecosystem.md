# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.93 — MassBank (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: MassBank is the world's premier open-access repository of high-quality reference mass spectra, contributed by research laboratories worldwide. Unlike MassIVE (raw experimental datasets) or GNPS (molecular networking), MassBank specializes in expert-curated reference MS and MS/MS spectra used for accurate metabolite identification, compound annotation, spectral similarity search, forensic analysis, environmental chemistry, food chemistry, and pharmaceutical research. For Bioquora, MassBank becomes the Reference Spectral Intelligence Layer, enabling AI reasoning from reference spectrum → compound → metabolite → pathway → disease → biomarker → therapeutic relevance.
