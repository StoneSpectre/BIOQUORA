# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.98 — AntiSMASH (Antibiotics & Secondary Metabolite Analysis Shell) (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: AntiSMASH, developed by the Medema Group, is the world's leading platform for automated biosynthetic gene cluster (BGC) identification and annotation. Unlike MIBiG (experimentally validated BGCs), AntiSMASH predicts potential BGCs directly from genome sequences, making it indispensable for microbial genomics, natural-product discovery, synthetic biology, and drug discovery. For Bioquora, AntiSMASH becomes the Automated Genome Mining Intelligence Layer, enabling AI reasoning from genome → predicted BGC → biosynthetic pathway → natural product → therapeutic potential.
