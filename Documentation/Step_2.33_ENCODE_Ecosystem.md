# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.33 — ENCODE (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: ENCODE (Encyclopedia of DNA Elements) is one of the world's largest functional genomics projects, funded by the NHGRI (National Human Genome Research Institute). It systematically maps regulatory DNA elements, transcription factor binding sites, chromatin accessibility, histone modifications, RNA expression, long-range chromatin interactions, and epigenomic regulation. For Bioquora, ENCODE becomes the Regulatory Genomics Intelligence Layer, enabling AI to reason from DNA sequence → regulatory element → gene expression → disease → therapeutics.
