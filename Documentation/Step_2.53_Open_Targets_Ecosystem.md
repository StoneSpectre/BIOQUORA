# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.53 — Open Targets Platform (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: Open Targets Platform, developed by the Open Targets Consortium (EMBL-EBI, Wellcome Sanger Institute, GSK, Sanofi, Biogen, Open Targets Foundation), is the world's leading platform for systematic therapeutic target identification and prioritization. Unlike DrugBank or ChEMBL, Open Targets integrates human genetics, GWAS, gene expression, CRISPR screens, pathways, protein interactions, literature, and clinical evidence to score target–disease associations. For Bioquora, Open Targets becomes the Target Discovery Intelligence Layer, connecting gene → protein → pathway → disease → evidence → therapeutic opportunity.
