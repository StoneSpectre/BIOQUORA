# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.92 — MassIVE (Mass Spectrometry Interactive Virtual Environment) (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: MassIVE, developed by the Center for Computational Mass Spectrometry (CCMS), University of California San Diego (UCSD), is one of the world's largest public repositories for raw mass spectrometry data. Unlike GNPS (analysis platform) or PRIDE (proteomics-focused repository), MassIVE serves as a general-purpose repository for proteomics, metabolomics, lipidomics, glycomics, natural products, microbiome, and multi-omics mass spectrometry datasets. For Bioquora, MassIVE becomes the Raw Omics Data Lake & Experimental Intelligence Layer, enabling AI reasoning from raw spectra → processed features → molecules → pathways → phenotypes → diseases → therapeutics.
