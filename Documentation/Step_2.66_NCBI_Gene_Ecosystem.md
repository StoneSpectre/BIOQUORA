# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.66 — NCBI Gene (Entrez Gene) (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: NCBI Gene (formerly Entrez Gene), maintained by the National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI), is one of the world's most authoritative resources for gene-centric biological information. Unlike HGNC (gene naming) or Ensembl (genome annotation), NCBI Gene integrates gene function, genomic location, RefSeq sequences, GeneRIFs, expression summaries, orthologs, bibliographic evidence, protein products, pathways, phenotypes, and disease associations. For Bioquora, NCBI Gene becomes the Central Gene Knowledge Intelligence Layer, connecting gene → transcript → protein → function → disease → literature → therapeutics.
