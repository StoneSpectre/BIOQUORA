# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.91 — GNPS (Global Natural Products Social Molecular Networking) (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: GNPS (Global Natural Products Social Molecular Networking), developed by the Center for Computational Mass Spectrometry (CCMS), University of California San Diego (UCSD), is the world's leading open platform for MS/MS-based molecular networking, spectral library search, natural product discovery, metabolite annotation, and collaborative metabolomics. Unlike HMDB (metabolite knowledgebase) or MetaboLights (experimental repository), GNPS specializes in mass spectrometry intelligence, enabling researchers to discover structurally related molecules, annotate unknown metabolites, and explore chemical diversity. For Bioquora, GNPS becomes the Mass Spectrometry & Molecular Networking Intelligence Layer, enabling AI reasoning from MS/MS spectrum → molecular family → metabolite → pathway → organism → disease → therapeutic opportunity.
