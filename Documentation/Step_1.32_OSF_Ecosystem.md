## STEP 1.32 — Open Science Framework (OSF) Ecosystem (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: Open Science Framework (OSF), developed by the Center for Open Science (COS), is one of the world's largest platforms for research project management, preregistration, datasets, software, protocols, notebooks, and collaborative science. For Bioquora, OSF is the research workflow and reproducibility layer.
