## STEP 1.25 — OpenCitations Ecosystem (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: OpenCitations is the world's leading open scholarly citation infrastructure, providing freely accessible citation graphs, bibliographic metadata, and persistent identifiers. For Bioquora, OpenCitations is a core component for building an explainable biomedical knowledge graph and citation intelligence engine.
