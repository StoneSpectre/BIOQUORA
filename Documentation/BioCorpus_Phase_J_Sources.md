## VIII. BIOQUORA MASTER INGESTION TARGET

Bioquora should continuously ingest and synchronize from:
* **Literature**: PubMed, Europe PMC, OpenAlex, Crossref, Semantic Scholar
* **Datasets**: GEO, TCGA, GTEx, ENCODE, SRA, Human Cell Atlas, UK Biobank, MIMIC-IV, PhysioNet
* **Structures**: PDB, AlphaFold DB, UniProt
* **Drugs**: DrugBank, ChEMBL, PubChem, BindingDB, Open Targets
* **Clinical**: ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP, FDA, EMA
* **Imaging**: TCIA, IDC, ISIC, BraTS, CheXpert
* **Ontologies**: UMLS, SNOMED CT, MeSH, HPO, Gene Ontology, Disease Ontology, ChEBI
* **Standards**: HL7 FHIR, DICOM, OMOP, GA4GH, CDISC

## FINAL ECOSYSTEM SCALE (PHASES A–J)

After Phases A–J, Bioquora's biomedical intelligence infrastructure encompasses approximately:
* **2,000+ authoritative websites, portals, repositories, and APIs**
* **500,000+ research papers** (indexed through literature sources)
* **1,000+ datasets and data portals**
* **200+ biomedical AI models**
* **100+ benchmark suites**
* **1,000+ ontologies and controlled vocabularies**
* **100+ international organizations and consortia**
* **100+ enterprise and cloud platforms**
* **50+ interoperability standards**
* **100+ workflow and bioinformatics ecosystems**

*This represents a near-complete map of the publicly accessible global biomedical research ecosystem and provides a strong foundation for Bioquora's knowledge graph, retrieval systems, AI training pipelines, and scientific reasoning infrastructure.*
