# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 1.24 — DOAJ (Directory of Open Access Journals) Ecosystem (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: DOAJ (Directory of Open Access Journals) is the world's leading curated index of high-quality, peer-reviewed Open Access journals. It is essential for Bioquora because it helps distinguish legitimate open-access journals from predatory publishers and provides trusted journal metadata.
