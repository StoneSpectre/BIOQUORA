# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.81 — Human Metabolome Database (HMDB) (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: The Human Metabolome Database (HMDB), maintained by The Metabolomics Innovation Centre (TMIC), University of Alberta, is the world's most comprehensive resource dedicated to human metabolites. Unlike ChEBI (chemical ontology) or KEGG (metabolic pathways), HMDB integrates metabolites, concentrations, biofluids, diseases, biomarkers, metabolic pathways, spectra (NMR/MS), enzymes, genes, microbiome metabolites, nutrition, exposome, and clinical information. For Bioquora, HMDB becomes the Metabolomics & Precision Medicine Intelligence Layer, enabling AI reasoning from gene → enzyme → metabolite → pathway → biofluid → biomarker → disease → therapy.
