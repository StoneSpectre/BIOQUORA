# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.61 — Orphanet (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: Orphanet, coordinated by INSERM (French National Institute of Health and Medical Research) and supported by a global consortium across Europe and partner countries, is the world's leading reference resource for rare diseases and orphan drugs. Unlike OMIM (gene-centric Mendelian disorders), Orphanet integrates rare disease epidemiology, ORPHA identifiers, orphan drugs, expert centers, registries, clinical practice guidelines, natural history, and healthcare resources. For Bioquora, Orphanet becomes the Global Rare Disease Intelligence Layer, enabling AI reasoning from gene → phenotype → rare disease → diagnosis → treatment → expert care.
