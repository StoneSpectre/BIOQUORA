# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.67 — RefSeq (NCBI Reference Sequence Database) (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: RefSeq (Reference Sequence Database), maintained by the National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI), is the world's gold-standard collection of curated genomes, chromosomes, transcripts, proteins, and non-coding RNAs. Unlike GenBank (primary archive of submitted sequences), RefSeq provides expert-reviewed, standardized, non-redundant reference sequences used by virtually every major biomedical resource. For Bioquora, RefSeq becomes the Reference Sequence Intelligence Layer, enabling AI reasoning from genome → transcript → protein → function → disease → therapeutic target.
