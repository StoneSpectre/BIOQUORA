# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 1.35 — FAIRsharing Ecosystem (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: FAIRsharing is the world's leading curated registry of data standards, databases, repositories, policies, identifier schemas, ontologies, reporting guidelines, and FAIR implementation resources. It is used by NIH, ELIXIR, EOSC, publishers, journals, and funding agencies. For Bioquora, FAIRsharing becomes the metadata governance and standards layer.
