# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.20 — Gene Ontology (GO) (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: Gene Ontology (GO) is the world's most widely adopted standardized vocabulary for describing gene and protein functions. Developed by the Gene Ontology Consortium, GO provides a common language that links genes, proteins, pathways, diseases, and experimental evidence across thousands of species. For Bioquora, GO is the Functional Intelligence Layer, allowing AI to understand what genes and proteins do, where they act, and which biological processes they participate in.
