# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.96 — NPAtlas (Natural Products Atlas) (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: NPAtlas (Natural Products Atlas) is the world's leading open knowledgebase of experimentally characterized microbial natural products, developed by researchers at the University of Alberta. Unlike PubChem (general chemistry) or DrugBank (approved drugs), NPAtlas specializes in microbial secondary metabolites, their producing organisms, chemical structures, biosynthetic origins, biological activities, and taxonomic distribution. For Bioquora, NPAtlas becomes the Microbial Natural Products & Biosynthetic Intelligence Layer, enabling AI reasoning from biosynthetic gene cluster (BGC) → natural product → chemical scaffold → biological activity → therapeutic target → disease.
