# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## PHASE E — ADVANCED BIOMEDICAL RESEARCH ECOSYSTEM

This phase focuses on specialized biomedical repositories, national infrastructures, consortium datasets, and high-value research portals that many developers overlook but are extremely valuable for Bioquora.

---

### 1. Human Cell Atlas (HCA)
**Purpose**: Single-cell atlas of the human body, scRNA-seq, Spatial biology, Cell annotations.
* **Official**: [Human Cell Atlas](https://www.humancellatlas.org)
* **Portal**: [HCA Data Portal](https://data.humancellatlas.org)

### 2. HuBMAP (Human BioMolecular Atlas Program)
**Purpose**: Human tissue atlas, Spatial transcriptomics, Single-cell datasets, Multi-omics integration.
* **Official**: [HuBMAP Consortium](https://hubmapconsortium.org)
* **Portal**: [HuBMAP Data Portal](https://portal.hubmapconsortium.org)
*Note: Provides thousands of multimodal and spatial datasets with interactive visualization and standardized processing pipelines.*

### 3. CELLxGENE
**Purpose**: Single-cell expression atlas, Interactive visualization, Downloadable datasets.
* **Official**: [CELLxGENE Discover](https://cellxgene.cziscience.com)

### 4. Single-Cell Multiomics Data Portal
**Purpose**: Integrated portal aggregating datasets from Human Cell Atlas, CELLxGENE, GEO/SRA, PanglaoDB, CDCP, and more.
* **Official**: [Single-Cell Multiomics Data Portal](https://scdb.openomics.org)
*Note: Aggregates thousands of datasets across epigenomics, spatial omics, proteomics, and perturbation studies.*

### 5. Allen Institute
**Resources**: Brain Atlas, Cell Atlas, Human Brain Atlas.
* **Official**: [Allen Institute](https://alleninstitute.org)
* **Portal**: [Allen Brain Atlas](https://portal.brain-map.org)

### 6. Human Protein Atlas
**Purpose**: Protein expression, Pathology, Tissue Atlas, Cell Atlas, Cancer Atlas.
* **Official**: [Human Protein Atlas](https://www.proteinatlas.org)

### 7. Human Cell Landscape
* **Official**: [Human Cell Landscape](http://bis.zju.edu.cn/HCL)

### 8. NeMO Archive
**Purpose**: Neuroscience datasets, Single-cell brain atlas.
* **Official**: [NeMO Archive](https://nemoarchive.org)

### 9. BRAIN Initiative
* **Official**: [NIH BRAIN Initiative](https://braininitiative.nih.gov)

### 10. Human Connectome Project
* **Official**: [Human Connectome Project](https://www.humanconnectome.org)

### 11. National Microbiome Data Collaborative (NMDC)
**Purpose**: Metagenomics, Metaproteomics, Metabolomics, APIs, FAIR microbiome data.
* **Official**: [National Microbiome Data Collaborative](https://microbiomedata.org)
* **Docs**: [NMDC Documentation](https://docs.microbiomedata.org)
*Note: Provides standardized microbiome datasets, APIs, and FAIR data infrastructure.*

### 12. Human Microbiome Project
* **Official**: [Human Microbiome Project](https://www.hmpdacc.org)

### 13. MicrobiomeDB
* **Official**: [MicrobiomeDB](https://microbiomedb.org)
*Note: A curated microbiome discovery platform included within the NIAID Data Ecosystem.*

### 14. Metabolomics Workbench
* **Official**: [Metabolomics Workbench](https://www.metabolomicsworkbench.org)
*Note: NIH recognized as the National Metabolomics Data Repository supporting raw data, processed data, metadata, analysis, and visualization tools.*

### 15. MetaboLights
* **Official**: [MetaboLights](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metabolights)

### 16. GlyGen
* **Official**: [GlyGen](https://glygen.org)

### 17. GlyCosmos
* **Official**: [GlyCosmos](https://glycosmos.org)

### 18. LipidMaps
* **Official**: [LIPID MAPS](https://www.lipidmaps.org)

### 19. Indian Biological Data Centre (IBDC)
**Purpose**: India-specific biomedical infrastructure (INDA, ISDA, IMDA, IPD, IBIA).
* **Official**: [Indian Biological Data Centre (IBDC)](https://ibdc.dbt.gov.in)
*Note: India's national repository for publicly funded life-science data, hosting specialized portals for nucleotide, structural biology, metabolomics, proteomics, and imaging data.*

### 20. NIAID Data Ecosystem
* **Official**: [NIAID Data Ecosystem Discovery Portal](https://data.niaid.nih.gov)
*Note: Provides a unified discovery layer across infectious disease repositories and resource catalogs.*
