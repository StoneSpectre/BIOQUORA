# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## PHASE G — ELITE BIOMEDICAL RESEARCH INFRASTRUCTURE

This tier encompasses the elite repositories and infrastructures used by organizations like DeepMind, Microsoft Research, Broad Institute, EMBL-EBI, Stanford, Harvard, NIH, and major pharmaceutical companies. These are among the most valuable resources for building Bioquora.

---

### 1. NIH Common Fund Data Ecosystem
* **Official**: [commonfund.nih.gov/dataecosystem](https://commonfund.nih.gov/dataecosystem)
* **Resources**: HuBMAP, Metabolomics, Glycomics, GTEx, Human Cell Atlas, Bridge2AI, ExRNA, LINCS, Kids First, SPARC, MoTrPAC.

### 2. Bridge2AI
* **Official**: [bridge2ai.org](https://bridge2ai.org)
* **Purpose**: Biomedical Foundation Models, AI-ready datasets, FAIR biomedical data, Multimodal learning.

### 3. SPARC (NIH)
