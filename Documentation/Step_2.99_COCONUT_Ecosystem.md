# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.99 — COCONUT (Collection of Open Natural Products) (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: COCONUT (Collection of Open Natural Products) is the world's largest open-access database of natural product chemical structures, integrating hundreds of thousands of natural compounds from numerous public databases. Unlike NPAtlas (microbial metabolites) or MIBiG (biosynthetic gene clusters), COCONUT focuses on chemical diversity, molecular structures, physicochemical properties, taxonomic origins, and drug-discovery potential. For Bioquora, COCONUT becomes the Universal Natural Product Chemistry Intelligence Layer, enabling AI reasoning from natural compound → molecular scaffold → biological source → bioactivity → therapeutic application → drug discovery.
