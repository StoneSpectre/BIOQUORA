# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.42 — InterPro (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: InterPro, maintained by EMBL-EBI, is the world's largest integrated resource for protein families, domains, repeats, motifs, functional sites, signatures, and Gene Ontology annotations. Rather than being a standalone database, it integrates more than a dozen expert protein signature databases into a unified annotation system. For Bioquora, InterPro becomes the Protein Functional Annotation Intelligence Layer, connecting protein sequence → domains → molecular function → biological process → disease → therapeutics.
