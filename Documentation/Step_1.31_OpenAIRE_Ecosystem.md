## STEP 1.31 — OpenAIRE Ecosystem (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: OpenAIRE (Open Access Infrastructure for Research in Europe) is one of the world's largest Open Science infrastructures, funded by the European Commission. It interconnects publications, datasets, software, projects, grants, organizations, researchers, repositories, and research outcomes. For Bioquora, OpenAIRE is the federation layer that links nearly every major research infrastructure into one interoperable graph.
