# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.85 — MetaCyc (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: MetaCyc, developed and maintained by SRI International, is the world's largest experimentally validated metabolic pathway database. Unlike KEGG (which includes computationally inferred pathways) or Reactome (focused mainly on human biology), MetaCyc emphasizes experimentally verified metabolic pathways, enzymes, reactions, compounds, and taxonomic distributions across all domains of life. It serves as the reference knowledgebase behind Pathway Tools and the BioCyc collection. For Bioquora, MetaCyc becomes the Curated Metabolism & Metabolic Engineering Intelligence Layer, enabling AI reasoning from gene → enzyme → reaction → metabolite → pathway → organism → disease → biotechnology.
