# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.39 — AlphaFold Protein Structure Database (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: AlphaFold Protein Structure Database (AlphaFold DB), developed by Google DeepMind and EMBL-EBI, revolutionized structural biology by predicting the 3D structures of hundreds of millions of proteins with unprecedented accuracy. It bridges protein sequence → protein structure → function → interactions → drug discovery. For Bioquora, AlphaFold DB becomes the Structural Biology Intelligence Layer, enabling AI-powered reasoning over protein structures, molecular interactions, enzyme mechanisms, and therapeutic design.
