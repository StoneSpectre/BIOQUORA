# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.45 — KEGG (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: KEGG (Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes), developed by Kyoto University Bioinformatics Center, is one of the world's most influential biological knowledgebases. Unlike Reactome, KEGG integrates genes, proteins, enzymes, metabolites, drugs, diseases, pathways, genomes, orthologs, glycans, reactions, and molecular interaction networks into a unified computational framework. For Bioquora, KEGG becomes the Metabolism & Molecular Systems Intelligence Layer, enabling AI reasoning from gene → protein → enzyme → metabolite → pathway → disease → drug.

### 2. Core Collections (Harvest EVERYTHING)
* **KEGG PATHWAY**: Metabolism, Signal Transduction, Immune System, Cancer, Neurobiology, Cardiovascular, Endocrine, Digestive, Infectious Disease, Cell Cycle, Apoptosis, Autophagy, Development, Drug Resistance, Microbiome.
* **KEGG GENES**: Human genes, Mouse, Rat, Zebrafish, Yeast, Bacteria, Viruses, Plants, Fungi, Thousands of organisms.
* **KEGG ORTHOLOGY (KO)**: Ortholog groups, Functional annotation, Evolutionary relationships, Comparative genomics.
* **KEGG COMPOUND**: Metabolites, Small molecules, Lipids, Amino acids, Sugars, Coenzymes, Natural products.
* **KEGG DRUG**: FDA drugs, Investigational drugs, Drug classes, Targets, Mechanisms.
* **KEGG DISEASE**: Genetic diseases, Cancer, Infectious diseases, Neurological diseases, Metabolic disorders, Cardiovascular diseases, Rare diseases.
* **KEGG REACTION**: Enzymatic reactions, Metabolic reactions, Transport reactions, Catalytic reactions.
* **KEGG ENZYME**: EC Numbers, Catalytic activity, Substrates, Products, Reaction mechanisms.
* **KEGG GLYCAN**: Glycan structures, Carbohydrate metabolism, Glycobiology.
* **KEGG BRITE**: Hierarchical classifications, Protein families, Drug classifications, Disease classifications, Taxonomy.
