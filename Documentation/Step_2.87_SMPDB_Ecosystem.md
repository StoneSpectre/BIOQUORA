# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.87 — SMPDB (Small Molecule Pathway Database) (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: SMPDB (Small Molecule Pathway Database), maintained by the University of Alberta / The Metabolomics Innovation Centre (TMIC), is one of the world's most comprehensive databases dedicated to human small-molecule pathways. Unlike KEGG (general pathways) or Reactome (molecular reactions), SMPDB specializes in human-specific metabolic pathways, disease pathways, drug action pathways, signaling pathways, and physiological pathways with highly detailed visualizations. For Bioquora, SMPDB becomes the Human Pathway & Therapeutic Intelligence Layer, enabling AI reasoning from metabolite → pathway → disease → drug → clinical intervention.
