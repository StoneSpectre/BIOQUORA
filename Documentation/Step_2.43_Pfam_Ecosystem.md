# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.43 — Pfam (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: Pfam is one of the world's most influential protein family databases, built around profile Hidden Markov Models (HMMs). Maintained historically by EMBL-EBI and now integrated into InterPro, Pfam provides curated protein domains, clans, domain architectures, evolutionary relationships, seed/full alignments, and HMM profiles. For Bioquora, Pfam becomes the Protein Domain Evolution Intelligence Layer, enabling AI to infer protein function, evolution, structure, and molecular mechanisms.
