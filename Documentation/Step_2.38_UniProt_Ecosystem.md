# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.38 — UniProt (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: UniProt (Universal Protein Resource), maintained by the UniProt Consortium (EMBL-EBI, SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics, PIR), is the world's definitive resource for protein sequences, protein function, domains, post-translational modifications (PTMs), protein interactions, pathways, disease associations, taxonomy, and proteomes. It serves as the central hub connecting nearly every molecular biology database. For Bioquora, UniProt becomes the Universal Protein Intelligence Layer.
