# BIOQUORA FOUNDER BIBLE

## STEP 2.97 — MIBiG (Minimum Information about a Biosynthetic Gene Cluster) (God Mode Resource Vault)

> **Importance**: MIBiG (Minimum Information about a Biosynthetic Gene Cluster) is the world's authoritative repository of experimentally validated biosynthetic gene clusters (BGCs), maintained by the MIBiG Consortium. Unlike AntiSMASH (prediction) or NPAtlas (natural products), MIBiG contains manually curated, experimentally confirmed BGCs responsible for producing natural products such as antibiotics, anticancer agents, antifungals, immunosuppressants, pigments, and toxins. For Bioquora, MIBiG becomes the Biosynthetic Gene Cluster Intelligence Layer, enabling AI reasoning from genome → biosynthetic gene cluster → enzymes → metabolite → biological activity → therapeutic application.
